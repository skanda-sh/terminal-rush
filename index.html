How scoring works

Points per line: Each correctly typed character earns 10 points. A speed bonus of up to 50 points is awarded for fast submissions (under 4 seconds). Your current combo multiplier (1× base + 0.25× per combo level, max 3×) scales the total.

Incorrect submissions earn 2 points per correct character with no bonus and reset the combo.

WPM = (correctly typed characters ÷ 5) ÷ elapsed minutes. Only counts characters from correct submissions.

Accuracy = correct characters across all attempts ÷ total characters typed, shown as a percentage.

Abort the current run? Your progress will be lost.
